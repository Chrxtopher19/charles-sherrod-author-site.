A Psychological Horror & Supernatural Thriller

A rainy night I dreamed
I’d taken my last breath,
and went from there it seemed,
Beyond the Realms of Death.
Chris's reality fractures when he loses his beloved Sarah. Terrifying visions invade his waking mind as he develops volatile, unexplainable extrasensory abilities. While local police hunt for logical answers, a cold, dark dimension rips straight through the fabric of reality, unleashing restless spectral entities and a trail of brutal occurrences. When homicide detectives uncover a disturbing video of a botched seance, the traditional investigation collapses, forcing the lead detective to employ professional paranormal investigators. Joining forces with Chris and his friend Becky, these experts realize a malevolent force beyond the veil targets Chris. To uncover the grim truth behind Sarah’s disappearance, they must survive a chilling descent, destroy literal demons, and battle an evil entity in a desperate exorcism before the darkness claims Chris's soul.

Poetry & Selected Writings

Whispers

The wind off of the stormy seas
Whispers pain through the trees
The rain before it hits the ground
Whispers pain without a sound
The mourning song of the birds
Whispers pain without words
Flitting wings, the butterfly
Whispers pain to the sky
A rolling wave onto the land
Whispers pain into the sand.
The wind off of the peaceful seas
Whispers love through the breeze
The rain before it hits the ground
Whispers love without a sound
A morning song without words
Whispers love from the birds
As beautiful as the butterfly
Whispers love to the sky
The endless wave onto the sands
Whispers love into my hands.

The Quiet Void

The Quest

Alone I walk upon this beach
The other side, I hope to reach
Before the dawn I sailed away
With hopes of finding love someday
Clouds of doubt rain overhead,
Tears of pain reign in my head
Through the storms I hold the oars
By breaking waves and rocky shores
Like eyes that shine into the night
A lighthouse spins its warning light
"Stay away! Your boat, your heart
No love here, don't even start."
though I sail this lonely ocean
Full of fear and raw emotion
In my craft named 'wishful thinking.
Like the sun, I think I'm sinking.
Will I find those loving hands
That live upon those golden sands?
Or could this be a quest forlorn
In search of one who wasn't born?"

The Author Behind the Words

Charles Sherrod is an independent author specializing in dark fiction, tense psychological horror, supernatural thrillers, and evocative poetry.

With a deep appreciation for rich atmosphere, compelling mysteries, and exploring the unknown, his work probes the thin lines separating reality, dreams, and the supernatural.
